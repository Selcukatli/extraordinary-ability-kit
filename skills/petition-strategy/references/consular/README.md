# Consular annexes

Immigrant visa procedure is largely uniform. What differs by post is the appointment
system, local civil-document requirements, language services, and how often administrative
processing is triggered.

One annex per post. `turkey-ankara.md` is the worked example showing the expected shape —
it is not a statement about any particular applicant. `TEMPLATE.md` is the blank.

**If there is no annex for your post, that is the normal case.** Do not generalize from
another country's annex. Go to the embassy's own consular pages, work through the
checklist in `TEMPLATE.md`, and — if you are willing — contribute the annex back.

Every annex must carry a `last-verified` date. Appointment systems, phone numbers, and
document requirements change without notice, and a stale annex is worse than none.
