# Consular Annex — Turkey (U.S. Embassy Ankara)

**Processing Location:** U.S. Embassy in Ankara processes all immigrant visas for Turkey.

**Key Details:**
- As of January 1, 2025, the consular section provides services in English, Turkish, Farsi, and Arabic
- Beginning November 1, 2025, NVC schedules applicants in their place of residence (or country of nationality if requested)
- All applicants must attend interviews with originals of all required documentation
- Registration required via the official Visa Appointment Services website or by calling:
  - From Turkey: +90 850 390 1950
  - From the U.S.: +1 313 572 0883

**Administrative Processing:**
- Some visa applications require further administrative processing after the interview
- This takes additional time (typically resolved within 60 days)
- Applicants are advised of this requirement after their interview
- More common for applicants from certain countries or in certain technical fields

**Country-specific considerations:**
- Turkish applicants may face administrative processing delays given regional security considerations
- Military service obligations should be resolved before the interview
- Turkish police certificates are obtained from the Turkish National Police (Emniyet Genel Mudurlugu)
- All Turkish-language documents require certified English translations
