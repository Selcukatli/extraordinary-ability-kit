Report case status.

1. Invoke the `petition-status` skill.
2. Read `case-overview.md`, each `<petition>/evidence-list.md`, the criterion files, the
   letters directory, and the petition narrative.
3. Report: current stage, criteria coverage, blockers split into what the applicant
   controls versus what they are waiting on, and time remaining against the filing target.
4. Rank next actions by what would most change the outcome — not by what is easiest.
5. If the filing target is not achievable, say so directly.

Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS`
