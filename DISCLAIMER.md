This project provides general reference material about U.S. immigration law and tools for
organizing documents and producing drafts. It is **not legal advice** and is not a
substitute for advice from a licensed attorney.

Immigration law is fact-specific. Guidance that is correct for one applicant can be wrong
for another with a similar profile. Nothing here has been reviewed against your particular
circumstances, because nothing here knows them.

## The author is not an attorney

This project was written by a petitioner documenting their own process, not by an
immigration lawyer, and it has not been reviewed by one. It reflects one person's

## Accuracy is not guaranteed

Immigration rules, fees, processing times, adjudication standards, and agency policy change
frequently and sometimes without notice. Content here reflects a point in time and may be
out of date, incomplete, or wrong.

Facts with a short shelf life are collected in
`skills/petition-strategy/references/volatile.md` with a verification date. **Verify
anything you will file or pay on against primary sources** — [uscis.gov](https://www.uscis.gov),
[travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov), and the Federal Register — before acting.

## AI-generated output requires human review

This software is designed to be driven by large language models. Models make confident,
plausible, and wrong statements. They misremember regulations, invent citations, and
produce text that reads authoritative while being inaccurate.

**Every draft, argument, and factual claim produced with this software must be reviewed by
a human, and legal content must be reviewed by a licensed immigration attorney before
filing.** Do not submit generated text to a government agency without that review.

This software deliberately does not fill out forms.
